# AI Policy

This document records how AI tools were used in the **Extreme Years** project, as declared in the project milestone reports (see `milestones/`).

## Milestone 2: Data Preprocessing and Initial EDA

Source: `milestones/InfoViz Milestone #2.pdf`

1. It was used to understand how to derive the columns for the column calculations such as `irradiation_ratio`, `above_half_peak_time` and relative starlight.
2. Assisted with the Matplotlib syntax for plotting the charts.

## Milestone 3: D3 Visualizations

Source: `milestones/InfoViz Milestone#3.pdf`

1. For Q1, AI (Codex, GPT-6) was used to assist in coding in terms of applying brushing technique to histograms and linking the plots.
2. For Q2, AI (Codex, GPT-6) was used in assisting with implementation of top N selection of lollipop charts and for deriving irradiation ratio (R) from eccentricity.
3. For Q3, AI (Claude Opus 5.5) was used to get help with calculating relative starlight. This involves help with the Kepler equation for identifying position on orbit given time of year and formula for relative starlight.
4. For Q4, AI (Claude Opus 5.5) was used to understand how to code a beeswarm graph, specifically the simulation section. AI help was also enlisted in the Q4 heatmap to draw the orange burst line.
5. For Q5, AI (Codex, GPT-6) assisted with box-plot whiskers and heatmap percentages linked to the slider.
6. For Q6, AI (Codex, GPT-6) assisted with uncertainty bars, shared-scale orbit drawings, and explanation drafts.
